# Privacy Policy for Kanun

**Effective date: 10 August 2026**

Kanun respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Kanun on supported Apple devices.

## 1. Contact

Kanun is an independently developed game.

For privacy questions, support requests, or requests concerning personal information, contact:

**Email:** [blowix@proton.me](mailto:blowix@proton.me)

## 2. Information collected by Kanun

Kanun does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer or to a developer-operated server.

Kanun does not use:

- User accounts
- Analytics services
- Advertising networks
- Tracking technologies
- Developer-operated cloud storage
- External gameplay servers
- Third-party data-collection SDKs

Information such as game settings, player names, statistics, achievements, cosmetic selections, tutorial progress, recent match information, and saved progress is stored locally on your device.

This locally stored information is not accessible to the developer.

## 3. Nearby multiplayer

Kanun includes nearby multiplayer functionality.

When nearby multiplayer is used, limited technical and gameplay information may be exchanged directly between participating devices over the local network. This may include:

- Player display names
- Match and session identifiers
- Gameplay commands and match state
- Information required to discover and connect to a nearby game

This information is used only to establish and operate the nearby multiplayer session.

It is not sent to the developer, stored on a Kanun server, used for advertising, or used to track players.

## 4. Local network permission

Kanun may request permission to access your local network when you choose to use nearby multiplayer.

This permission is used only to discover, host, and join nearby Kanun games.

You can deny or later disable this permission through your device settings. Solo play and same-device multiplayer do not require local network access.

## 5. Information stored on your device

Kanun may store the following information locally:

- Game preferences
- Audio and haptic settings
- Player display names
- Gameplay statistics
- Achievements and records
- Cosmetic selections
- Tutorial and guidance progress
- Daily Shot history and preferences
- Saved progress
- Recent match information

This information remains on your device.

You can remove locally stored information through the reset options available in the app. Uninstalling Kanun also removes its locally stored information, subject to the normal behaviour of your device and any backups managed by you or Apple.

Kanun does not maintain a developer-operated remote copy of this information.

## 6. Daily Shot reminders and notifications

Kanun may offer optional Daily Shot reminders.

These reminders use local notifications scheduled on your device. Kanun does not use a developer-operated notification server to send Daily Shot reminders.

Notification permission is optional and can be denied or changed at any time through your device settings.

## 7. Game Center

Kanun optionally supports Apple Game Center for features such as leaderboards and achievements.

If you choose to use Game Center, gameplay information required for those features, such as leaderboard scores and achievement progress, may be transmitted to and processed by Apple through Game Center.

Game Center is an Apple service and operates under Apple's terms and privacy practices. Kanun does not operate Game Center or maintain a separate copy of your Game Center account information on a developer-operated server.

Kanun does not use Game Center information for advertising, analytics, or tracking.

## 8. Support communications

When you contact Kanun by email, you may voluntarily provide information including:

- Your email address
- Your name, if included
- The contents of your message
- Technical details or screenshots you choose to provide

This information is used only to:

- Respond to your request
- Investigate a reported problem
- Provide technical support
- Maintain necessary support records
- Protect the security and integrity of the app

Support communications are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and any related follow-up, unless a longer retention period is required for legal, security, or dispute-resolution purposes.

You may request deletion of a support communication by emailing [blowix@proton.me](mailto:blowix@proton.me).

## 9. Apple services

Kanun is distributed through Apple's App Store and uses certain Apple platform services.

Apple may independently process information relating to App Store downloads, purchases, payments, refunds, device diagnostics, Game Center, or other Apple services under Apple's own terms and privacy practices.

Kanun does not receive your complete payment-card information.

This Privacy Policy describes Kanun's own data practices and does not govern information processed independently by Apple.

## 10. Third parties

Kanun does not share personal data with:

- Advertising networks
- Analytics providers
- Data brokers
- Marketing services
- External gameplay servers

Kanun may communicate with Apple services, such as Game Center, when you choose to use features that depend on those services.

If Kanun's data practices or third-party dependencies change in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated as appropriate.

## 11. Children's privacy

Kanun does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Nearby multiplayer information is exchanged directly between participating devices for the purpose of operating the game and is not collected by the developer.

Optional Game Center features are provided through Apple and are subject to Apple's account, privacy, and platform controls.

Parents or guardians with privacy concerns may contact [blowix@proton.me](mailto:blowix@proton.me).

## 12. Security

Kanun is designed to minimise data processing and avoid unnecessary transmission of information.

Nearby multiplayer and optional Apple services use platform-provided technologies. However, no electronic communication or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 13. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information you provide directly to the developer through support communications, including the right to:

- Request access to your information
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your information
- Restrict or object to certain processing
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
- Submit a complaint to an applicable data-protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact [blowix@proton.me](mailto:blowix@proton.me).

Because Kanun does not operate user accounts or remotely store gameplay information on developer-operated servers, the developer cannot access, retrieve, correct, or delete information stored only on your device or information controlled independently by Apple services.

## 14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Kanun's features, legal obligations, or data practices change.

The updated policy will be published at the same URL with a revised effective date.
